# Chore — Support

Welcome to the official support page for **Chore: Stay On Top**, your reminder companion.

Chore helps you organize everyday tasks with simple, accessible reminders.

## Getting Started

Open Chore and create a new task. Choose whether it repeats daily, weekly, monthly, or does not repeat.

You can manage your existing tasks within the app and customize reminder preferences in Settings.

## Notifications

Chore uses local notifications to remind you about tasks and check-ins.

If notifications aren't appearing, open your iPhone's Settings, find Chore, and verify that notifications are allowed.

You can also review your reminder preferences inside Chore.

## Accessibility

Chore supports VoiceOver and is designed to provide an accessible experience for users who rely on Apple's screen reader.

## Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or assistance, open Chore and navigate to **More → Get Support**.

## Privacy

Chore stores your task information locally on your device and does not collect personal information.

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://grimlin238.github.io/chore-privacy-policy/).
