# Chore — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 6, 2026**

Chore: Stay On Top ("Chore") is a task and reminder management application designed to help users organize daily, weekly, monthly, and one-time responsibilities.

### Information Collection

Chore does not collect personal information from its users. The app does not require account registration and does not collect information for advertising or analytics.

### Local Data Storage

Chores, reminder schedules, and related information are stored locally on the user's device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to external servers operated by the developer.

### Notifications

Chore uses Apple's local notification system to provide task reminders and daily and monthly check-in notifications.

Notification permissions can be managed through the device's Settings application.

### Data Management

Users can manage and delete their saved chores within the application. Removing the application may also remove its locally stored information, subject to Apple's device backup and restoration behavior.

### Third-Party Services

Chore does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

### Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Chore's functionality or data practices change.

### Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the support contact information provided on Chore's App Store listing.
